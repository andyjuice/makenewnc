---
title: Our Beliefs
summary: >
  The Holy Bible is the inspired word of God and is the basis of our
  statement of faith.
body: |
  Accordingly, we believe that there is one God, trinitarian in nature, and one path of understanding, instruction, and salvation for all mankind: the person of Jesus Christ.

  In accordance with the Bible, salvation of souls is the primary mission of the church. Any and all other causes, however apparently worthy, must always remain subordinate to this mission.

  Therefore, the church shall, if possible, avoid political endorsements and associations. Similarly, any/all social causes will be pursued only to the degree that doing so aligns with the mission of preaching the Gospel.
bullets:
  - One God, trinitarian in nature
  - Salvation through Jesus Christ alone
  - Gospel proclamation as the church's primary mission
---
